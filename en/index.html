Who is Roddi Health for?

For owners of rats, hamsters, guinea pigs, rabbits, chinchillas, degus, mice and other small pets that need attentive health care.

Why I’m building Roddi Health

It all started with a love of rats 🐭❤️

When two rats came into my life, I started an Instagram blog to share their adventures. Along with the joy came health worries: vet visits, medication, symptoms, weight changes. I was constantly writing it all down or trying to keep it in my head.

So I’m building the app I need myself, together with other rodent owners.
