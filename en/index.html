What Roddi Health can do

The four things that cause the most stress, all in one place.

  1. 01Never miss a doseSet up a course of treatment once and reminders arrive on time. Mark each dose with one tap. Reminders for vet visits, weigh-ins and cage cleaning work the same way.
  2. 02See how their health changesLog symptoms and weight, and the app charts the trend. That makes it easier to spot changes in time.
  3. 03Arrive at the vet with the full historyVisits, prescriptions, current and past treatment and test results in one pet profile. No more trying to remember at the appointment.
  4. 04Everything at handNotes, health details and quick search: find what you need in seconds, even with several rodents.

Why I’m building Roddi Health

It all started with a love of rats 🐭

When two rats came into my life, I started an Instagram blog to share their adventures. Along with the joy came health worries: vet visits, medication, symptoms, weight changes. I was constantly writing it all down or trying to keep it in my head.

“So I’m building the app I need myself, together with other rodent owners.”
