Privacy Policy

Updated November 19, 2026

Who we are

Roddi Health is an app for rodent health that is currently in development. The roddihealth.app website is run by Uliana and Veronika Dykhtiar, the founders of the project (“we”). We are responsible for processing your personal data. For any question about your data, write to roddihealth@gmail.com.

What data we collect and why

  • Email. You leave it in the early access form. With it we store the date and time, the form and page you signed up from, the site language and the fact of your consent (the version of its text). Why: to write to you about the launch and invite you to early access, and from time to time to send app news and useful materials about rodent care, so you do not forget about us. Legal basis: your consent.
  • Answers to the short survey (optional): which pets you have and how many, when you last gave medication, what phone you use (iPhone or Android), whether you want to test the app, and a comment. They are linked to your email. Why: to understand what rodent owners need and plan the launch. Legal basis: your consent.
  • Emails to us. If you write to our address, we keep the correspondence so we can reply.
  • Technical data. Our host automatically keeps service logs (IP address, browser, time of request). They are needed for security and for the site to work, and we do not analyze them.

We do not collect health data about people. Answers about your pets are linked only to your email.

Cookies and analytics

We use Microsoft Clarity to see how visitors use the site (heatmaps and session recordings) and to improve it. Clarity sets cookies and recognizes you between visits only if you have allowed analytics in the banner on the site. Without your consent it works in a limited mode without cookies and does not link your visits together. We do not use advertising cookies.

Your choice is kept in your browser. You can change it at any time with the “Cookie settings” link in the site footer.

Who we trust with data

We do not sell your data or share it for advertising. To run the site we use services that process data on our behalf:

  • Vercel: website hosting;
  • Google: Sheets and Apps Script (where sign-ups are stored) and Google Fonts (the site’s typefaces);
  • Microsoft: Clarity, see above;
  • jsDelivr: the content delivery network the style library is loaded from.

These services may process data on servers in different countries, including the United States.

How long we keep data

We keep your email and survey answers until you withdraw consent or ask us to delete them, but no longer than needed for the launch and the mailing. We keep correspondence as long as it is needed to reply.

Your rights

You can ask at any time to see, correct or delete your data, restrict its processing, get a copy of it or withdraw your consent. Withdrawing does not affect the lawfulness of processing before it. Write to roddihealth@gmail.com and we will reply within a month.

If you believe we are violating your rights, you can complain to a supervisory authority: in Ukraine, the Ukrainian Parliament Commissioner for Human Rights; in the EU, the data protection authority of your country; in the UK, the Information Commissioner’s Office. We process data in line with Ukrainian personal data protection law and, for visitors from the EU, the EEA and the UK, the GDPR.

How to unsubscribe

Every email will contain an unsubscribe link. You can also write to us and we will remove your address from the list.

Children

The site is not intended for children under 16. If you believe a child has left us data, write to us and we will delete it.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of the page.
