About our blog articles and disclaimer

Updated 16 November 2026

How we prepare articles

Our blog articles are original overviews written by the Roddi Health editorial team. We read material from vets, veterinary organisations, universities and animal welfare organisations, compare it and set out the facts in our own words and our own structure. Where sources disagree, we show it.

We do not copy, translate or retell other articles paragraph by paragraph. We take facts from several sources and list them under each article with direct links. Our articles are written in Ukrainian first; the English versions are our own translations of our own texts.

We use AI-based tools when preparing texts. Articles are not peer-reviewed veterinary publications unless this is stated next to a specific article.

Which sources we choose

We rely first on veterinary associations and reference works, universities, government and specialist bodies, and peer-reviewed research. Material from animal welfare organisations and veterinary clinics is used as a supplement. If we cannot check a statement against a source, we do not publish it.

Medicines and doses

We do not give medicine doses or recommend medicines. Only a vet who has examined the animal can prescribe treatment.

Fact-check date

Each article shows when its facts were last checked against the sources. We review articles when recommendations change or when someone reports a mistake or a broken link.

Symptoms and emergencies

Articles about symptoms and emergencies carry a visible notice: if your animal’s condition gets worse, see a vet straight away. These articles help you understand what may be happening and what to tell the vet, but they do not let you diagnose your animal at home.

Articles on other sites

Every card in the blog leads to one of our own articles. If we ever add a card that leads to an article on another website, it will be marked “article on an external site”, and its text will be our own short description, not a quotation.

This is not veterinary advice

Roddi Health is not a veterinary clinic and does not provide veterinary services. Blog articles are for information only and are not a diagnosis, a treatment plan or a recommendation about medicines or doses. They do not replace an examination by a vet.

Before making any decision about your animal’s health, talk to a vet and check the information yourself. If your animal is unwell, don’t delay getting help because of something you read on this site.

Limitations

We try to keep our articles accurate, but we do not guarantee that they are complete, up to date or suitable for your animal. Recommendations change and sources may disagree. You make your own decisions about caring for your animal. To the extent permitted by law, Roddi Health is not responsible for the consequences of using this information.

Sources we mention

Organisations and websites are named for reference only. They are not affiliated with Roddi Health and did not write or endorse our texts. The owners of external websites are responsible for their content.

Photos and copyright

Blog photos come from Wikimedia Commons and have free licences. Each photo shows its author, licence and a link to the file.

Mistakes and requests

If you find a mistake or believe that something on the blog infringes your rights, write to roddihealth@gmail.com: we will check it and correct or remove it.
